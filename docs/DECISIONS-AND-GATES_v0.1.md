(5) The title tracker is **record only**: it never changes the unit, the contract or ownership. (6) The **General Manager confirms the deed handover**. (3) Discounts are measured per unit against its listed price: a **Sales Manager approves up to 5 %**, the **General Manager above 5 %**; the preparer cannot approve. (4) Installment terms come from a **contract profile, with the number of months adjustable from 12 to 36**. (5) A quotation is **valid 7 days** unless changed.
